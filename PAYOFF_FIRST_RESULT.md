# First complete payoff example

Sample accounts only — these are not your balances or payment instructions.

Starting debt: **$1,500**, split between a $1,000 card at 24% and a $500 card at 12%. Both plans have the same **$250 monthly payment target**.

| Plan | Interest | Total paid | Debt-free date |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Highest interest first | $74.18 | $1574.18 | August 31, 2026 |
| Smallest balance first | $93.43 | $1593.43 | September 30, 2026 |

Highest interest first costs **$19.25 less** in this example. Both plans meet every modeled minimum and keep the cash floor and reserves protected.

The final payment is August 20. A remaining fraction of a cent rounds to zero at the August 31 statement close; the planner waits until then to call the debt paid off. Earlier small payments also cover trailing interest.

The sample assumes no new borrowing or fees, simple daily interest on a 365-day basis, explicit month-end statements, and same-day payment credit/debit/settlement. Future minimums are capped at the balance and use the greater of $25 or 1% of balance plus posted interest.

Sample checking starts at $400, with a $100 floor and $50 reserved. Explicit sample income is $1,200/month and bills $900/month, supplied through the extra 90-day cash horizon. These are illustrative assumptions, not estimates of your household.
