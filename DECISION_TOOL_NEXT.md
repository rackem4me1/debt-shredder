# Decision-tool direction

The user authorized a change in direction on October28 after reporting a new
automated financial system. Begin with a system-coverage and lower-NET-pay stress
report, rather than another comprehensive budget app or a public subscription
service. The reported personal baseline is private and is not an engine-ready
bank snapshot. Existing saved plans describe their original inputs.

## First milestone

Produce one plain-language report with: declared current automations, proposed
changes, unverified items, uncovered obligations, and results of explicit net-pay
scenarios when opening cash, protected funds, pending items and event dates are
known. Use the existing per-account liquidity engine, preserve transfer lifecycle
and reserves, and show what inputs/uncertainties constrain the conclusion. Do not
label annual-average budget sufficiency as proof of dated cash-flow safety.

The first read-only weekly report now exists at /weekly-check. It compares
allocations and a flexible goal; it is not the dated cash projection described
above. Next connect reconciled balances, protections and dated events to the
existing liquidity engine. Avoid a parallel ledger or replacing existing records
before reconciliation. Reuse confirmed bill inputs with source/review states.

## Follow-on milestones

1. Promo deadline planning, after exact end date and true-zero vs deferred-interest
terms are confirmed. Current wizard adapter does not support promotional cards.
Do not silently convert the balance into ordinary-rate debt.
2. Extra loan-payment comparisons, after principal/note-rate/interest mechanics
and treatment of extra payments are confirmed. A disclosed APR is not silently
substituted for a contractual note rate; original loan amount is not current debt.
3. Validate usefulness with a few users before planning hosting, subscriptions or
a broad mortgage/credit-maintenance feature set. Existing local software is not
a ready multi-user commercial product.

## Boundaries

Bank execution stays outside this tool. No inferred completed payments, payroll
splits, transfers or rent funding. Gross hourly earnings do not establish net
pay. Omitted housing is unresolved rather than zero. Statement-balance card
payments remain cash obligations; avoid counting both the purchase and statement
payment as two cash expenses. Track one-time reimbursable spending separately,
without treating an expected reimbursement as already available money.

Unknown contractual mechanics and promotional inputs remain explicit. Mortgage
readiness and credit maintenance are deferred; no optimum or underwriting claim.
No broad redesign of the frozen schema or calculation engine is authorized by
this reference step.

## Private baseline

local_scenarios/decision_baseline_2026-10-28/ holds the user's original reported
notes, structured reference and readiness assessment. Received date is not a
verification date. Keep all three out of source checkpoints and public examples.
The new notes supersede earlier narrative assumptions, but do not overwrite the
old account/bill/paycheck files or assert that planned changes took effect.
